Ad Campaign

Campaign description

BNC FieldHouse is a sports bar and grill focused on game-day energy, social experiences, and a memorable local atmosphere. Our campaign is designed to increase awareness and engagement among Iowa State students, local sports fans, and University alumni.

The message is centered on the campaign hooks “Wednesday Wings and Music Bingo” and “50 Cent Wings,” which create urgency, excitement, and repeat-visit appeal while staying aligned with BNC’s brand personality.

Campaign breakdown

  • Targeting: Adults 21–35 in Ames, sports fans, students, alumni
  • Primary goal: Increase brand awareness and engagement
  • Hook: Wednesday Wings + Music Bingo / 50¢ Wings
  • Budget: $1,500 over four weeks

Targeting Criteria

Who this campaign speaks to.

  • Iowa State University students and alumni
  • Local adults interested in sports and nightlife
  • People who enjoy bar food, beer, and cocktails
  • Consumers planning social hangouts and group outings
  • Fans of college and professional sports
  • Users looking for game-day dining experiences
  • People browsing restaurants and event-based promotions
  • Mobile users who respond to direct offers and visual urgency
