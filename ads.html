Ad Campaign

Built to reach the right audience and convert casual interest into visits.

Campaign description

BNC FieldHouse is a sports bar and grill that blends game-day energy, local dining, and a social atmosphere. The campaign focuses on driving awareness and encouraging trial among local students, sports fans, and young adults who value food, drinks, and lively events.

Our strategy centers on a clear value proposition: great food, strong atmosphere, and a place where people can meet up before, during, and after games. The ad creative uses a bold, energetic visual tone and keeps the messaging simple and direct.

Campaign breakdown

  • Targeting: Age 21–34, local sports fans, college students, nightlife audience
  • Goal: Increase awareness and reservations
  • Hook: Game-day energy + crowd favorite food
  • Benchmark: CTR 3–5%, conversion rate 2–4%

Targeting Criteria

Who this campaign speaks to.

  • Local residents in Ames and surrounding areas
  • Students and alumni connected to Iowa State
  • Sports fans and event-driven social audiences
  • People looking for casual dining and late-night plans
  • Young adults seeking group hangout experiences
  • People browsing for bars, food, and entertainment
  • Visitors planning game-day or weekend outings
  • Mobile users who respond to direct offers and urgency

Creative Strategy

What makes the ads work.
