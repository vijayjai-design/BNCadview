Executive Summary

We built a digital campaign and branded experience for BNC FieldHouse.

This semester, we created a conversion-focused marketing system for a local sports bar and grill, including strategic audience targeting, ad creative, landing-page design, and campaign planning. The goal was to turn interest into action by increasing awareness, driving reservations, and building a stronger digital presence for the business.

Strategy Overview

Turning interest into action.

The goal of the project was to create a digital marketing experience that matched BNC FieldHouse’s vibe: energetic, local, and built for social engagement. We focused on the audience most likely to visit regularly: college students, young professionals, sports fans, and local nightlife seekers.

Our work combined audience targeting, creative direction, and conversion-focused messaging so the campaign would feel consistent across every touchpoint.

Key deliverables

  • Audience targeting strategy
  • Offer and messaging framework
  • Four display ad variants
  • Landing page continuity and CTA flow
  • Conversion-focused website structure

Performance Goals

Our campaign benchmarks.

3-5% estimated CTR
2-4% landing page conversion target
25-40% increase in local awareness

Key Takeaways

What this project taught us.

The most important part of digital marketing is creating a clear path from attention to action. This project showed how strong visual branding, practical messaging, and a focused landing page can work together to drive real user engagement.
