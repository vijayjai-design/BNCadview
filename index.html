Executive Summary

Our campaign helps BNC FieldHouse grow awareness and foot traffic.

This project focuses on developing a comprehensive display advertising campaign designed to promote our business, BNC Fieldhouse, and increase its overall brand awareness. Throughout the semester, our group applied key marketing and digital advertising concepts to develop a campaign strategy that includes identifying a target audience, establishing campaign goals, creating compelling advertisements, and designing a dedicated landing page.

Campaign Summary

Turning digital interest into local foot traffic.

Our campaign incorporates industry benchmarks such as CPM, CTR, and CVR to estimate the advertising budget and measure potential performance. By researching media costs and expected results, we were able to plan for efficient media spread and a realistic project outcome.

We created four distinct ad variations including a medium rectangle, a leaderboard, a wide skyscraper, and a half page, maintaining consistent messaging throughout the process. Each ad was designed to support the campaign’s goal of increasing brand awareness and engagement.

Key campaign deliverables

  • Target audience definition
  • Campaign goals and benchmark estimates
  • Four ad variations for different placements
  • Dedicated landing page focused on conversion
  • Responsive GitHub website for campaign presentation
